Before you glove up

  • Strap or tight grip. A wrist strap, or the phone held hard in your fist, screen facing in.
  • An arm's length clear all round. Nothing breakable, nobody close, no walls or screens in reach.
  • Keep your elbows soft. Don't snap them straight: punch to just short of full reach.
  • Stay 1.5 m from the screen with the camera, so both fists stay in the picture.
