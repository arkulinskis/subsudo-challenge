How the rankings work
  • Everyone starts at 1000. Ranked bouts move your rating up or down, more when the result is a surprise (Elo, like chess).
  • Knockouts move it 25% more, split decisions 20% less. Bouts against guests and the house give points but don't touch your rating.
  • #1 in the world holds the World belt; #1 in each city holds that city's belt.
  • Points: win 25, KO win 35, draw 12, loss 5; house win 15 (+ bonus); bag work 1 (5 a day).
