Three rounds to the board

No sparring partner in the room. No timezone to match. Just your round against theirs.

Glove up

Hold your phone in your fist (right hand, the bell, then left) or stand back from your laptop camera. Every jab, hook, uppercut and block is counted, with its speed.

Send the round

Call out a fighter from any city, pin an open call-out to the board, or send a friend a link. Your score stays sealed in their Mail.

Let the judges talk

They fight back on their own time. Three judges score power, volume and ring craft. KO, decision or draw: your rating and the city board move.

What's in the gym bag

Phone in fist

The motion sensor tells punches from blocks: forward is a punch, up or down is a block, up and forward is an uppercut, a fast turn is a hook. The phone rings the bell and calls the hand switch.

Laptop camera

A hand tracker running in your browser follows both fists. The video never leaves your device; only the punch log is sent.

Bouts by mail

No scheduling. A call-out waits 7 days in their Mail. Duck it, and it shows on your fighter card.

City and world boards

A rating like chess: beat stronger fighters, climb faster. #1 in your city holds the city belt; #1 overall holds the World belt.

Fight friends without accounts

Send a link. They fight back from their own phone. If they join later, the bout moves onto their record.

House fighters

Nobody to fight tonight? Coach Bruno, Big Dee and the gym crew send challenges, with bonus points for beating them.

Questions from the locker room

Is it safe?

It's shadow boxing. Clear space around you, grip the phone hard (a wrist strap helps), and never punch people, walls or screens. You train at your own risk; stop if anything hurts.

How accurate is the punch counting?

Good, not lab-grade. Speeds are estimates from your phone's motion sensor or the camera, and everyone is measured the same way. The server scores every round again from its punch log.

Can people cheat by shaking the phone?

Shaking isn't boxing: the judges reward both hands, variety, blocks and combos, not just noise. Odd-looking rounds are flagged for the admins.

Is my camera video uploaded?

No. The picture stays in your browser. Only the punch log is sent: when, which hand, which punch, how fast.

Who sees my data?

Other members see your ring name, city, record and bouts. Never your email. The Privacy & Terms page has the details.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Subsudo is free.
